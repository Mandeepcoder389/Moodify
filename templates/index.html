HOW MOODIFY WORKS

Three steps.
One better playlist.

MOODIFY keeps the experience straightforward. You decide when the camera is used, and the recommendations come from a CSV file you can edit.

01

Open your camera

Allow camera access to see live expression estimates. Small frames are processed by your local Flask app.

YOUR CONTROL
02

Estimate expression

OpenCV locates a face and DeepFace estimates the most prominent visible facial expression.

COMPUTER VISION
03

Find matching songs

The expression maps to a mood category, and matching tracks are selected from your local CSV library.
