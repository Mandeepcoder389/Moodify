BEHIND THE EXPERIENCE / 03

Music discovery with
a human rhythm.

MOODIFY is a simple music-discovery project that turns an expression estimate into song suggestions.

OUR IDEA

Music can meet
the moment you're in.

MOODIFY gives you a camera preview, an expression estimate, and songs from a music library you can update. The suggestions are for discovering music, not for deciding how someone really feels.

WHAT MOODIFY CAN DO

Music discovery, made simple.

01

View an expression estimate

Start the camera to see live expression estimates. You choose when to start and stop.

02

Find songs for a mood

Capture a frame to match its estimated expression to a music category.

03

Explore your library

Browse suggested tracks and see which music categories are available.

04

Make it your own

Update the song library with your favourite tracks, artists, genres, and languages.

THE PIPELINE

From a live frame
to a song suggestion.

  1. Browser sampling Start the camera to see a live preview and expression estimates.
  2. Image validation When you capture, the app checks the image before analyzing it.
  3. Face and expression The app finds a face and estimates its most visible expression.
  4. CSV matching The expression estimate maps to a music category, and matching songs are shown.
PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

Clear about what the model can do.

Camera access: The browser requests permission. While live analysis is active, a downscaled frame is sent to this app after each analysis completes. Press Stop to end the camera and analysis.

Local processing: On localhost, frames are analyzed on your computer and are not saved to disk or sent to a cloud service by this project.

Not a diagnosis: Facial-expression models can be wrong. They do not know your inner feelings, personality or mental-health state. Treat results as an experimental estimate, not a fact.

Third-party links: Song search links open YouTube in a new tab. MOODIFY does not stream or host copyrighted music.
